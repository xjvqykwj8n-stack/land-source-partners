Common questions

What landowners ask us.

Am I obligated to accept an offer?

No. Sharing your property information and receiving an offer does not obligate you to sell.

Do I need to clean up the property first?

No. We evaluate land as it is. Tell us what you know, and we’ll handle the research.

How does closing work?

When possible, a reputable title company or real estate attorney handles the transaction so documents and funds are processed professionally.

What types of land do you consider?

We consider vacant residential lots and other undeveloped land. Location, access, zoning, utilities, and local demand all factor into our review.
